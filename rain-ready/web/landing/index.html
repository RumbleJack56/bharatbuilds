    What it does

    From a photo to a checked fix

    Every step writes a row in the task history with who, when and the photo. A job counts as confirmed only after a different resident has looked.

    Report

    A resident sends a photo and a location from the in-app camera, in under a minute.

    Find the owner

    The report is linked to the nearest drain within 150 m. The owner agency is named, or the result says “No owner found”.

    Rank

    Tasks are ordered by the hours of fill time that cleaning would buy at the reference rain.

    Confirm

    A different resident confirms or disputes the work. Whoever did the job cannot confirm it.

    Who uses it

    Who it is for, and what they see

    Resident

    Reports in under a minute, sees what happened to the report, and confirms finished work.

    Official

    Sees only their agency’s tasks, ranked, with an overdue badge. Accepts, sends back as “Not ours”, or completes with an after-photo.

    Ward coordinator

    Assigns tasks that have no owner and generates the ranked plan.

    Anyone

    The public board shows agency scores and the action log without a login.

    Rain simulator

    Compares pocket fill times now and after cleaning. Illustrative parameters. A scenario, not a forecast.

    Rain capture

    Store, detain or recharge, decided by rule for each roof. Experimental. Not reviewed by a hydrogeologist. Not for construction.
