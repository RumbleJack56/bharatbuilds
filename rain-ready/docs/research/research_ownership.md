# Rain-Ready: who owns which drain in Delhi (research, 2026-10-10)

VERIFIED legend: OPENED = I downloaded/fetched the source and read the quoted text. SNIPPET = only a search-result summary, not opened. FAILED = tried, reason given.
Downloads kept in scratchpad/ownership/ (NGT PDFs, DHC order, DMC Act, IDMC report).

## 0. Headline findings
1. The "60 ft" rule is real, but the primary-source wording is about ROADS (Right of Way >= 60 ft vested in GNCTD/PWD by notification dated 10.01.2012) and about drains ALONG those roads (SDMC's affidavit to NGT). Your uncited "60 ft (18 m)" is right; "wide" should be read as Right of Way, not carriageway.
2. Ownership does NOT cleanly follow the road. Delhi High Court (2024) says drains are split among PWD, I&FC, MCD, NDMC, DDA, DSIIDC, Cantonment Board, and a single drain can have several agencies. The Court itself said in May 2026: "there is confusion as to which agency is in-charge of which drain".
3. There was no unification of all drains. Only 22 Yamuna-outfall open drains (+ Kushak, Sunheri Pulla, partly) went to I&FC in 2024. On 29 May 2026 the High Court chose a coordination cell (IDMC as Special Task Force), not a single owner.
4. No public drain-level list with owner exists that I could find on a government portal. PWD has a drain map page (empty when fetched). The best drain-level lists with an "agency" column are in NGT filings (IDMC report 2020), zone-coded but partial.
5. The 2024 Rajinder Nagar incident: reporting says MCD (Karol Bagh Zone) JE sacked and AE suspended; MCD said drains were covered by encroachers. I found no authoritative statement of legal ownership of that drain. Keep "No owner found" / "MCD (inferred, news)" with care.

## 1. The 60-foot rule

### 1a. GNCTD notification 10.01.2012 (roads >= 60 ft RoW vested in GNCTD) - OPENED (via PWD's NGT reply)
- Quote: "the Government of NCT of Delhi issued a notification dated 10.01.2012 whereby the roads having 'Right of Way' of 60 feet and above belonging to the MCD were vested with the Govt. of NCT of Delhi" and "necessary directions were issued to the engineering wing of the MCD to hand over the said roads to the concerned engineers of the applicant department."
- Source: Reply on behalf of PWD, GNCTD, OA 559/2022 Sanjeev Jain v. MCD, NGT Principal Bench, dated 12.07.2023 (affidavit by EE Vishnu Avtar Gupta).
- URL: https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/REPLY BY R-8 PWD GNCTD IN OA NO. 559 of 2022 SANJEEV JAIN VS MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF DELHI & ORS.pdf (spaces need %20)
- Notes: The notification itself (Annexure 1) and the road inventory (Annexure 2) are scanned images in the PDF; I did not read them (no OCR available). The reply covers roads only, not drains, and the example is civil/electrical/horticulture.
- Also: PWD Feb 2025 status presentation (per search snippet only, SNIPPET): roads of "60 ft RoW and above, transferred from MCD in January 2012", about 1,400 km. Could not locate the file itself. Candidate: https://pwddelhi.gov.in/writeread/Circular\Cir_202502271545457660.pdf returned HTTP 400 on curl (FAILED). Treat as unverified.

### 1b. Drains follow roads - SDMC affidavit to NGT, Feb 2020 - OPENED
- Quote: "all drains along with all roads having Right of Way more than 60 ft. in SDMC area are constructed, maintained and cleaned/de-silted by the PWD since May 2012." and "all drains along with all roads having Right of Way less than 60 ft. are constructed, maintained and cleaned/de-silted by SDMC only."
- Also: "SDMC has approximately 247 nos. of drains with length 164 Km. more than 4 ft. in depth/width" and "approximately 28000 nos. of drains with length of 5604 Km. below 4 ft. in depth/width". Also lists other authorities: "Central Public works department, Public works department of Govt. of Delhi, Delhi Development Authority, Railway Authorities, Delhi Cantonment Board, New Delhi Municipal Council etc."
- Source: Response on behalf of SDMC, OA 06/2012 Manoj Mishra v. UoI (NGT), dated "February 2020" (day blank).
- URL: https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Affidavit by SDMC in OA No. 06 of 2012.pdf (use %20; plain spaces gave 403)
- Caveat: This is South Delhi MC's statement about its own area, a party's affidavit, not a government order. Karol Bagh zone was in North Delhi MC at the time; no equivalent NDMC(North) affidavit read. Since Dec 2022 the three corporations were re-unified into one MCD, so the rule is presumed the same but I have no unified-MCD statement of it.
- Exception seen in primary data: IDMC report table lists a drain on "60 ft. road, PWD drain" owned by PWD (row 63, Chattarpur Mandir Nalla, M-IV/SZ). Same table lists "Phirni Nallah... NH8 drain" as CPWD and "Zonapur... Kachcha drain" as I&FC, and "Sumpwell... SDMC". So the table supports multiple owners by road type.

## 2. Agency map and published numbers

### 2a. Chief Secretary chart quoted by Delhi High Court (the best single source) - OPENED
- Quote: "the major storm water drains of about 3740.31 km in Delhi are managed by various agencies, under the jurisdiction of various governments. While PWD and I&FC Department under GNCTD manage 2064.08 km and 426.55 km, respectively, the local body MCD manages 521.87 km, NDMC under Government of India manages 335.29 km, DDA under GoI manages 251.30 km, DSIIDC a corporation under GNCTD manages 98.12 km and Delhi Cantonment Board under GoI manages 39.68 km and others manage 3.42 km."
- Check: the figures sum to 3740.31 (I added them).
- Also: "even in case of such Drains, there are multiple agencies involved in the maintenance and operations of a single drain. For example, management of water regulators ... is managed by I&FC Dept. but pumping of sewage at such times is managed by MCD." and "Management of drain(s) by multiple agencies leads to blame game between agencies".
- Source: Delhi High Court, W.P.(C) 7594/2018 & 9617/2022 (Court on its own motion v. UoI), order of 8 April 2024, quoted at paras 8-9 inside the I&FC report in NGT OA 257/2024 (dated 18.12.2024).
- URL: https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/REPORT BY IRRIGATION AND FLOOD CONTROL DEPTT, GNCTD IN OA NO. 257 of 2024 Dr. Lokesh Kumar Vs. Govt. of NCT of Delhi.pdf
- Caveat: "major storm water drains" only; excludes small colony drains. Note how it compares: SDMC alone claimed 5,604 km of sub-4-ft drains plus 164 km of larger ones (2020), so the 3,740 km is not the full network. Delhi's whole network per the 2025 Drainage Master Plan is 18,958 km (Tribune, below).

## 3. Changes 2024-2026

### 3a. HC order 8 April 2024: single agency for 22 Yamuna outfall drains - OPENED (quoted in I&FC report)
- Quote (I&FC report paras 3-5): "during the 17th meeting of Integrated Drain Management Cell (IDMC) held under the Chairmanship of Chief Secretary, Delhi on 18.04.2024, it was decided ... that responsibility of all 22 open drains, which are out falling in river Yamuna, should be handed over to Irrigation & Flood Control (I&FC) Department, GNCTD as a single agency/department." and "I&FC Department ... would be the sole department/ agency responsible for management and operation of all the twenty-two (22) open drains". "de-silting work ... for the monsoon season of the year 2024 would be continued to be undertaken by the departments under whose control these open drains existed".
- MCD Commissioner affidavit (OA 06/2012, EA 05/2024): "22 drains + 2 drains (Kushak drain and Sunheripulla drain) have already been handed over to I & FC ... in the wake of the objective that a single agency should deal with a drain for proper accountability. However, I&FC had apprehensions about taking over the covered portion of Kushak and Sunheripula drain and did not take over the covered portion." OPENED. URL: https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Affidavit by Commissioner, MCD in OA No 06 of 2012 WITH EA No. 05 of 2024 IN OA No. 06 of 2012 (Nizamuddin West Association Vs. UOI & Ors.).pdf (date not captured; 2025).
- Impact for pilot: these are the big Yamuna-outfall open drains (Najafgarh, Barapullah, Shahdara...). They are not the street-side drains in Old Rajinder Nagar.

### 3b. Delhi High Court, 29 May 2026, W.P.(C) 17064/2024 Ravinder Yadav v. GNCTD - OPENED
- Quote (para 2, quoting order of 23 May 2026): "Presently, there is confusion as to which agency is in-charge of which drain and which sewer line. It is seen in some places that the same drain is partially under the control of multiple agencies which completely derails proper working and maintenance."
- Holding: IDMC Cell "can function as the STF"; DUSIB and DSIIDC added; IDMC to "oversee and supervise the issues relating to waterlogging, drain management, sewage management..." meeting every Friday for three months; periodic reports to the Court. GNCTD itself had proposed a Special Task Force of CE-level officers from PWD, IFCD, DJB, DSIIDC, DUSIB, DPCC, Forest, DDA, MCD, NDMC, DCB "to resolve territorial jurisdictional issues". Court said "creation of multiple committees or agencies would not serve the purpose". Next listing 30 June 2026.
- PWD status report in same order: "444 intersection points of PWD Drains with MCD Drains, of which intervention is required at 298 points... 113 intersection points of PWD Drains with IFCD Drains, of which intervention is required at 46 points" and PWD has "17 Divisions" in 3 zones.
- URL: https://delhihighcourt.nic.in/app/showFileJudgment/PMS29052026CW170642024_160615.pdf (downloaded OK).
- Conclusion: as of June 2026 there is NO unified owner; a coordination cell instead. Status after 30 June 2026 (hearing) not found. A 2026 monsoon NGT filing (I&FC, 7 July 2026, 77 drains) appears only in a search snippet.

## 8. Cases that must stay "No owner found"
1. Any road whose RoW is not in PWD's road list/RoadCSR record (including all nine named pilot roads until checked individually).
2. Drains on roads where RoW is between about 50-70 ft or unknown/disputed.
3. Covered drains, drains under footpaths/ramps, drains at junctions between a PWD road and an MCD lane (PWD report: 444 PWD-MCD intersection points, 113 PWD-I&FC), and any drain where two agencies share sections (HC: "same drain is partially under the control of multiple agencies").
4. Drains on DDA, Railways, DMRC, NHAI, CPWD, Cantonment land without a source naming the drain.
5. Sewers (DJB) shown as storm drains; the map must distinguish sewers (DJB) from storm drains.
6. The specific drain in the 2024 Rajinder Nagar incident: only press attributions (MCD Karol Bagh zone officers punished); no court/government finding of legal ownership read.
7. Anything about last-desilted dates (no public drain-level data).

## 9. Suggested UI wording
"Owner by road rule: PWD (road RoW >= 60 ft, per GNCTD notification 10 Jan 2012) - rule-based, not a drain register" and show the confidence. Always display that the Delhi High Court (29 May 2026) recorded "confusion as to which agency is in-charge of which drain".
