## Insights and actions

**19 places were listed in three or more different years. 14% of all location-dates fall on them, and 71% of listed rows have a single date.** Repeat failures at the same place point to a drain that is not being kept clear, not to bad luck.

- Action (inspect): Inspect the inlets and the connecting drain at each chronic place before the monsoon and photograph the result. Route each place to the owner shown, or to the coordinator when none is found.

**Only 50% of location-dates sit on a road that can be matched to the PWD inventory by name. 316 places have no owner that public records can assign.** A task with no owner has nobody to accept it. The 2012 notification and the 2020 affidavit assign drains by road width, and that needs the road to be known.

- Action (clarify_owner): Ask MCD, PWD and I&FC to name the owner for each unresolved recurring place, and publish the answer with its source.

**In the pilot ward, New Rohtak Road at Anand Parbat (foot of Zakhira Flyover) (18 dates; 1% of Delhi's listed places have as many) and Bagga Link / Jhandewalan roundabout (Dayal Chowk) (10 dates; 1% of Delhi's listed places have as many) recur most. Old Rajinder Nagar low point (Bazaar Marg) is in no list.** The pilot places are not unusual among Delhi's recurring places, so the ward plan applies the same rules the city needs.

- Action (plan): Work the ward action plan in order. Places no list covers enter through resident reports.

**88% of the 1,100 recorded location-dates fall between June and September; July alone holds 45%.** Cleaning that finishes after the first heavy July rain misses most of the recorded events.

- Action (schedule): Finish the inspection and cleaning round for every place on the chronic list by the end of May, and record each job in the action list so residents can confirm it.

**Five days hold 41% of all listings. The median day lists 3 places, and 46 of 156 days list one.** Most listings come from a few extreme days. A place listed on an ordinary day is failing at modest rain.

- Action (prioritise): Rank places by listings on ordinary days first. Keep extreme-day response (pumps, diversions) separate from drain cleaning.

**The 10 busiest roads hold 45% of location-dates out of 257 listed roads. Ring Road alone holds 13%.** A long corridor needs a corridor-level review, since fixing single points leaves the rest of its run unchanged.

- Action (coordinate): Ask the owners along each top corridor for one joint inspection plan, with one named contact per segment.

**The 2022 list carries dates from 2017 onward and the three lists miss 3 row numbers. 1 row has no date and 7 rows have no place text.** Counts are close but not exact. The lists record places that disrupted traffic, so interior lanes are missing.

- Action (note): Read every count as at least that many. Add resident reports for places the lists do not cover.
