    Where the drain points come from

    OpenStreetMap has no roadside drains here. Points sit on a drain route described in the 1976 plan, on a real OSM line, or on a road with the drain marked as assumed. The three checks compare the 1976 description with the geometry.

    Owner rule used for roads: a road in the PWD inventory with a Right of Way of 60 ft (18.29 m) or more has its drains with PWD, from the 2012 notification and a 2020 affidavit to the NGT (a South Delhi statement applied here by inference). Trunk drains listed under MCD in the 1976 plan are shown as MCD. Every owner here is a documentary reading, not a field-verified fact.
